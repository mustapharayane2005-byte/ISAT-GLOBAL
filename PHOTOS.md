# Photography brief

Twelve slots. Drop a file into `public/images/` named exactly after the slot id
(`hero.webp`, `phase-1.webp`, and so on) and it replaces the placeholder frame on
the next `npm run dev` or `npm run build`. `.webp` is preferred; `.avif`, `.jpg`
and `.png` also work.

Shared direction for every frame: real Nigerian people and places, documentary
rather than staged, natural light wherever possible, no visible competitor
branding, no lens flare or heavy grading. Colour should stay true; the page
supplies the only saturated colour on screen, so the photography stays quiet
around it. Leave calm space in the frame where the composition allows, because
several images sit directly under large type.

## hero

- **Ratio** 16:9 · **Export** 2560 x 1440 · **Priority** yes, this is the LCP image
- **Prompt** Photorealistic wide editorial photograph, golden hour on a Lagos
  rooftop. A Nigerian woman in her late twenties in modern business-casual dress
  stands three-quarters to camera, phone held low, looking out over the city. The
  Lagos skyline recedes behind her and a slim 5G mast rises at the right third.
  Warm low sun raking from camera left, long soft shadows, gentle haze in the
  distance. Shot on a 35mm lens at f/2.8, subject sharp, skyline softly falling
  off. Natural skin tones, no colour cast, quiet sky with room for nothing above
  her head.

## 5g-home

- **Ratio** 16:9 · **Export** 2400 x 1350
- **Prompt** Photorealistic interior photograph, early evening in a middle-class
  Abuja living room. A child of about nine watches a film on a tablet on the sofa
  while a parent takes a video call standing near the window. A small white
  fixed-wireless router sits on a shelf in the mid-ground, unbranded. Warm lamp
  light mixed with cool blue dusk through the window. 28mm lens at f/4, whole room
  legible, slight motion in the parent's gesture. Lived-in, not styled.

## phase-1

- **Ratio** 4:3 · **Export** 2000 x 1500
- **Prompt** Photorealistic documentary photograph at blue hour on a Lagos
  rooftop. Two riggers in harnesses and hard hats align a panel antenna on a
  slim mast. City lights spread out far below and behind. Cool ambient sky, a
  single warm work light on the crew. 50mm lens at f/4, crew sharp, city bokeh.
  Real working posture, tools visible, no posing to camera.

## phase-2

- **Ratio** 4:3 · **Export** 2000 x 1500
- **Prompt** Photorealistic documentary photograph, midday on a red-earth road
  outside Kano. A fibre crew feeds duct into an open trench, a large orange cable
  drum in the near foreground. Flat hard overhead sun, dust in the air, scrub and
  low buildings on the horizon. 35mm lens at f/8, deep focus from drum to horizon.
  High-visibility vests, authentic equipment, no staged smiles.

## phase-3

- **Ratio** 4:3 · **Export** 2000 x 1500
- **Prompt** Photorealistic aerial photograph at sunrise over a small Nigerian
  town. Low rooftops and a market square in the valley, and on the ridge above it
  a solar-powered telecom tower with its panel array catching the first light.
  Long shadows, mist sitting in the low ground. Drone altitude roughly 120 metres,
  24mm equivalent, f/5.6, everything sharp. Calm, hopeful, unpeopled.

## isat-one

- **Ratio** 21:9 · **Export** 3360 x 1440 · Sits on a black section, so the frame
  should read dark edge to edge with no bright corners
- **Prompt** Photorealistic panoramic photograph looking straight down the cold
  aisle of a modern data hall at night. Black server racks line both sides in
  strict perspective, amber and orange status LEDs repeating into the distance, a
  single engineer in silhouette walking away from camera two thirds down the
  aisle. Only the rack lights and low floor wash for illumination. 24mm lens at
  f/2.8, one-point perspective, vanishing point centred. Deep blacks, no fill
  light, no lens flare.

## Sector rail

All six share a format: **4:5 portrait**, exported at **1600 x 2000**. They appear
side by side in a horizontal rail, so keep the subject centred and the crops
consistent in tightness. Treat them as one set, shot the same way.

### sector-manufacturing

Photorealistic interior photograph inside a Nigerian assembly plant. A worker in
a blue coverall stands at a machine holding a rugged tablet showing a line chart.
Cool overhead industrial lighting, machinery receding behind. 50mm lens at f/2,
worker sharp, factory falling into soft focus. Portrait crop, subject centred.

### sector-agriculture

Photorealistic outdoor photograph at sunrise in a cassava field. A farmer in his
fifties stands among the rows checking a phone, a small white soil-sensor post in
the near foreground. Warm low sun from behind, rim light on his shoulders, dew on
the leaves. 85mm lens at f/2.2, compressed rows behind. Portrait crop.

### sector-education

Photorealistic photograph inside a university lecture theatre in Ibadan. Students
at tiered desks with laptops open, one student in the mid-ground in focus
mid-note. Daylight through tall windows on the left, no flash. 35mm lens at f/2.8.
Portrait crop taken from the aisle, looking slightly down the tier.

### sector-healthcare

Photorealistic photograph in a Lagos hospital reading room. A clinician in
scrubs studies a chest scan on a large medical display, face lit by the screen.
Cool clinical ambient light, dark surroundings. 50mm lens at f/1.8, clinician
sharp against a dark background. Portrait crop.

### sector-government

Photorealistic documentary photograph at a public service counter in Abuja. A
uniformed officer and a citizen work through a form together at a terminal, both
in three-quarter view. Natural light from a high window, institutional interior,
no signage legible. 35mm lens at f/2.8. Portrait crop, candid.

### sector-retail

Photorealistic candid photograph at a busy Lagos market at midday. A trader in a
bright patterned wrapper accepts a phone payment from a customer across a stall
of produce. Vivid fabric colours, dappled light through canopy tarpaulins, crowd
blurred behind. 35mm lens at f/2.5. Portrait crop, energy and movement.
